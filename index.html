General Rules for Answering

  • ✓ Speak slowly and in short, clear sentences. An official interpreter will be present.
  • ✓ Answer only what is asked. Do not volunteer extra information.
  • ✓ Be consistent with your employment contract, job description and submitted documents.
  • ✓ Be honest and factual. SIRI checks genuineness of the job, salary, duties and recruitment.
  • ✓ If you do not understand: “Could you please repeat or explain the question?”
  • ✓ Never guess. Say: “I do not remember exactly / I need to check the contract.”
  • ✓ Focus on the current Sales Consultant role. Explain the previous Management Assistant application only when asked.
